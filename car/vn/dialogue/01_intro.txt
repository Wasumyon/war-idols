It doesn't matter how good or bad things get - even beneath the all-seeing red eye of Jupiter, everyone's gotta make a buck.
The boom times, when my pops moved out to the Jovian moons as a volunteer for the Giant Leap Pioneer Project, are long over.
The bubble dreams of our fathers popped. The lost children of that era lived as gig-economy freeters, junkies, and burnouts.
Many were gazing bleary-eyed at the end of a gunbarrel - until the invasion of Io.
But war is every fool's market, and whenever the bull comes to ride, sex and violence are top sellers.
There was plenty of the latter when the bugs came to Io. We even got some of the former when the lookieloos and digital nomads came.
The XO cut radio silence and started selling licenses for "civilian war journalism" to fund the war effort and keep morale up.
More for the Treasury Committee than for the actual soldiers and their families.
Streamers with scanty digital avatars did the best, no matter what any scholar or activist wanted to change about society.
Fresh meat looking to pay off mortgages and bad loans flooded the frontlines.
Everyone back home, too scared to dip in the blood, was nevertheless peckish for realistic, raw sadist or revenge footage.
My meal ticket came when one of these "Virtual Youtubers" wandered into the grinder armed with just her selfie stick.
Her JustFans wasn't paying the bills, and the war was perfect for her adrenaline-junkie nerves.
On a spit and a handshake, we became partners.
Our contract: I keep her breathing, and she shakes her big charisma for the audience. We cut it all fifty-fifty, profit and expense.
Death clause: all the belongings of the deceased transfer to the living member - after a Space Vultures Guild investigation confirms no foul play.
The moonchild of this beautiful relationship was our first thousand subs.
Donations with a message of support or snark - "hyperchats" - were now enabled. After a savage eighty percent cut for "taxes and services."
The low-hanging fruit was running out. Soon the crackpots and the spooks were hand in hand with the grunts in one symbiotic pool of entrepreneurship.
More and more rumblings of foul play and conspiracy started hitting the net - and making quick cash.
That said, the simplicity of war joined with the guarantee of return finally made the present clear, if not bright:
[i]Stay alive, and elbow out the competition with the wildest content you can get away with monetizing. Those are the rules of the War Idols.[/i]
