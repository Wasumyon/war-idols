Seven months since then. Four of those spent suspended on a torture rack, resetting my spine. Another two for rehab.

Only one other made it out of the verdigris hellpits of Chaac Patera. Three weeks later, he was gone too. I'm sorry I couldn't make it in time, buddy.

The bald recruiter with the bespectacled, beady eyes had them pegged on the leg, then my file, then my face, then quickly back to the file. Must have looked like hell.
Something catches his eye. He stiffens; his arm begins to snap to attention before he catches himself and coughs.

"First Loki Patera, Tarsus Regio, Prometheus, Uruk Sulcus, Second Loki Patera, Conamara Chaos, Argus Panoptes!" he whistles.
His tone goes somber again.
"Chaac Patera..."

He drops the papers.

"Sarge. What are you doing here?" He leans closer. "Someone put you to this?"
"Enlisting."
"You know what I- Look behind you. We're not short on our backlog. A record like this, you should be living high on the hog on your VA benefits!"

Pain pulses through my phantom knee as I wait in silence.

"Uh... right. I'm sorry, but with *that*, you know I can't do anything for you. But... you know Vulkan Security is always hiring. With this record, I'm sure you can skip the physical."

As I about-faced, the Triskelion Lion of Nemean Company - three heads swallowing Io, Europa, and Ganymede - sat emblazoned on my cloak. It commanded silence.
Nothing could be heard except the clack of my cane and boots.

The show only landed harder because of the stairs. For a moment I shifted my weight onto a leg that belonged to me, and then my cheek dug hard into the red pavement.
Turning over, it's watching me again. That mocking red eye of Jupiter. Well, laugh, damn you.

No one turns their head. People pass around me like a stream around stone. Good. A man who can't get up by himself is as good as dead on Io.

The boom times, when dad moved out to the Jovian moons volunteering for the Giant Leap Pioneer Project, are long over.
Our fathers' bubble dreams popped. The lost children of that era lived as sycophants, freeters, and burnouts.
Many dreamed, gazing bleary-eyed into bottles and gunbarrels - until the infestation of Io.
Don't matter how good or bad things get, under that all-seeing eye, everyone's gotta make a buck. Or get drunk and punch someone.

Can't remember the first two bars, or was it three? I had to get it out of my system. I had to win, even if it was Pyrrhic.
It felt good, getting cracked by that ducking left hook. He and I learned that the cane is not just for the aura.

War is a fool's market, and whenever the bull comes to ride: sex, drink, and violence are the top sellers.
And retards like me inevitably flow to Keno's gutter.

C: So was it loud? Like actually loud, or does it all just become white noise?

I try to endure the cacophony through an old-fashioned, to no avail.

C: Is it true what they say about the smell? How many people have you killed!?~

P: Lady, you've got no gun, no muscle, and no dictionary. What's a tart like you doing out here?

C: Content!

P: This the content you were looking for?

I can see the sweat bead on her brow as she raises her hands. Her smile curdles, but she holds it together. She presses her ribcage into my laspistol.

C: Oh, deary me! Chat, you see what rapscallions will do to a damsel out in these boondocks!? Constable! I need the thesaurus police!~

Eyes turn. Sweat beads on my brow. I move to put the gun away.
Like a viper, she catches my hand, thrusts her slim arm over my elbow, and turns her tiny back into my chest.
She holds my hand with the gun barrel up to her lips and snaps a selfie of her miming blowing smoke off it.

C: See! I know how to shoot too, hotshot.

I push the goblin off me, and she transfers the momentum into a spin and curtsies.

P: Get lost!

She didn't. She slid back into the stool beside me like we've known each other for years, thumb already scrolling.
On the Feed was the clip of us - our... dance. Notifications ring off her phone, reading off donations and comments.

C: Fifty. Seventy. Two-hundred! Oh~ Thank you, chat! Drinks are on me today, old man.

She taps in a bunch of drinks on the bar screen. And begins to swipe.

P: You saying I can't pay for my own?
C: I'm saying a *lady* sometimes feels generous.
P: Generous ladies are dangerous ones. 'Sides, a guy always pays for the first date.
