--- Intro ---

It doesn’t matter how good or bad things get, even beneath the all-seeing red eye of Jupiter, everyone’s gotta make a buck. The boom times when my pops moved out to the Jovian moons as a volunteer for the Giant Leap Pioneer Project are long over. The bubble dreams of our fathers popped and lost children of that era lived as gig-economy freeters, junkies, and burnouts. Many were gazing bleary-eyed at the end of the gunbarrel until the invasion of Io. 

But war is every fool’s market and whenever the bull comes to ride, sex and violence are top sellers. There was plenty of the latter when the bugs came to Io. We even got some of the former when the lookieloos and digital nomads came. 

The XO’s cut radio silence and started selling licenses for “civilian war journalism” to fund the war effort and keep morale up - more for the Terran Armed Services Fiduciary (Treasury or omit?) Committee than the actual soldiers and their families. The fad was streamers with scanty digital avatars did the best, no matter what scholar or activist wanted to change about society. 

Fresh meat looking to pay off mortgages and bad loans flooded the frontlines. Everyone back home too scared to actually risk dipping in the blood were nevertheless still peckish for realistic, raw sadist or revenge footage.

My meal ticket came when one of these “Virtual Youtubers” wandered into the grinder armed with just her selfie stick. Her JustFans wasn’t paying the bills and the war was perfect for her adrenaline junkie nerves. On a spit and handshake we became partners. Our contract stipulates the following: I keep her breathing and she shakes her big charisma for the audience - we cut it all 50/50 profit and expense. On the unfortunate circumstance of pain of death, all the belongings of the deceased will be transferred to the living member - after an investigation by the Space Vultures Guild that no foul play was involved. 

The moonchild of this beautiful relationship was our first 1000 subs. Donations with a message of support or snark (“hyperchats”) were now enabled on our channel - after a savage 80% cut was taken away for “taxes and services”. 

[However the low hanging fruit was running out and soon the crack-pots and spooks were hand in hand with the grunts in this symbiotic pool of entrepreneurship. More and more rumblings of foul play and conspiracy was starting to hit the net and making quick cash.]

That said, the simplicity of war joined with the guarantee of return finally at least made the present clear if not bright: stay alive and elbow out the competition with the wildest content you can get away with monetizing. That’s the game rules for the War Idols.
